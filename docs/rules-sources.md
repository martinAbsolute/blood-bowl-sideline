# Rules snapshot and provenance

Snapshot: `bb2025-2026-10-09`. Blood Bowl Third Season (2025). This is an independent community builder, not an organiser certification tool.

The 9 October audit is recorded in [regular-player stats](roster-stat-audit.md), [Star Player stats](star-stat-audit.md), [Sevens validation](sevens-rules-audit.md), and [Star Player eligibility](star-rules-audit.md). The characteristic fixtures retain source URLs and tests cover every catalog player. The known September snapshot remains readable because its schema and selection IDs are compatible. Device recovery drafts and new saves upgrade to the October version without losing selections, ownership metadata or revision checks. Reads of legacy cloud teams recompute legality under the corrected rules; newly invalid choices remain available to repair. Stored rows and historical league snapshots are not bulk rewritten. Unknown rules versions remain unsupported.

## Presets

Team special-rule help uses factual paraphrases of the BB2025 effects, cross-checked against [Blood Bowl Base: The Teams](https://bloodbowlbase.ru/bb2025/core_rules/the_teams/) and [Nuffle Zone: Special Rules](https://nufflezone.com/en/blood-bowl-news-en/special-rules-blood-bowl/). The Team Captain replacement timing follows the corrected commentary shown in the former reference. These descriptions and league reference pages do not change the saved ruleset validators, costs, or roster snapshot. League pages show league-affiliated and universal stars; god-specific eligibility remains in the shared team validator.

- **Default:** initial 1,000,000 GP draft budget, with additional skills priced at player-value costs. This is an editable exhibition draft; additional skills, stars, and inducements need organiser agreement for ordinary league recruitment. It does not establish a league team's match history or earned SPP.
- **Matched Play:** 1,150,000 GP; tier-based SP; one extra skill per player; at most four added copies of each individual elite skill, as clarified on page 3 of the [official May 2026 FAQ](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_20-05_blood_bowl_faq_errata-gytvlserev-ngihd3chox.pdf); tier-dependent secondary and star allowances. This differs from Sevens' two added elite skills total.
- **EuroBowl 2026:** BBTC revision `EB2026_04`, cross-checked with the Ukrainian builder: tier-based roster and skill-gold budgets, shared Flowing Funds, primary-only two-skill stacking, secondary and stack limits, restricted veteran/legend stars and advancement exclusions.
- **NAF World Cup 2027 v2.1:** the [organiser's v2.1 PDF](https://nafwc.com/app/uploads/NAF-World-Cup-Rules-V2.1.pdf), pages 2–4, was visually inspected on 9 October and confirms the existing BBTC `WC2027_03` / Ukrainian v2.1 data. All roster budgets, SPP, ordered advancement costs, stacking/star permissions, cumulative star tax, eleven regular hires before Stars and the Secret Weapon bribe cap match. The requested “World Cup 2026 v2.1” is labelled **2027** by the sources. Squad-wide Star uniqueness needs an organiser check across all six coaches.
- **Kyiv “Seven Sins” Sevens, Season 3:** organiser requirements supplied by the user establish the 650,000 GP budget, tier skill packages, no skill stacking, no Leader, no stars, and tier-2 captain advancement exclusion. [Warhammer Community's Sevens designer notes](https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/f0owm2ar/designers-notes-on-blood-bowl-sevens/) confirm the four non-Lineman limit, universal 100,000 GP rerolls, and mandatory Lineman Veteran. Spike! Journal 22 drafting details were cross-checked against its [community transcription](https://bloodbowlbase.ru/bb2025/spike_journal/issue_22/): 7–11 players, Veteran MA reduced by one, Sevens staff prices and caps, and the restricted inducement list including Desperate Measures. The Veteran's once-per-game ability covers failed pickup, catch or pass rolls, and an own Block that would knock the Veteran down. [NAF BB2025 Sevens recommendations](https://www.thenaf.net/naf-recommendations-and-clarifications-for-bb2025/), Sevens revision 26 September 2026, apply: Insignificant is ignored during drafting; Team Captain retains starting Pro and may also be the Veteran. The event's Leader ban overrides NAF's general matched-play permission. NAF gameplay clarifications remain reference guidance; this roster builder does not enforce pitch actions, dice resolution, stalling or match results. Sources inspected 6 October 2026; organiser decisions take precedence.

## What the checks cover

**Blood Bowl Sevens 2025 Edition:** the standard Spike! Journal 22 Matched Play draft, distinct from the Kyiv event: 600,000 GP, 7–11 players, four specialists, mandatory Lineman Veteran, and the Sevens staff and inducement costs above. Its four tiers grant 1/2/3/4 SP, with primary skills costing 1 SP and secondary skills 2 SP, at most one secondary, one added skill per player and two added elite skills. No Star Players appear in the eligible Sevens inducement list. Matched Play starts at zero Dedicated Fans, with up to five purchased at 20,000 GP each. NAF's clarification permits purchased Leader and Pro and retains Captain Pro. These rules cover a fixed Matched Play roster; they do not implement the distinct Sevens league advancement system. The current Sevens tiers were transcribed from Spike! Journal 22's [Sevens tier table](https://bloodbowlbase.ru/bb2025/spike_journal/issue_22/#sevens-team-tiers), not copied from the Kyiv event or the full-size game. Slann has no published GW Sevens tier and is excluded from this standard preset instead of assigning an invented tier; it remains supported by the other presets. The [GW designer notes](https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/f0owm2ar/designers-notes-on-blood-bowl-sevens/) and [NAF Sevens recommendations](https://www.thenaf.net/naf-recommendations-and-clarifications-for-bb2025/) were checked on 6 October 2026. The GW download page does not expose the current Sevens tier file to the research browser; the table and detailed Matched Play quantities were cross-checked against the community transcription of the published supplement.

Position limits, total players, Insignificant balance, shared Big Guy limits, skill access/categories/elite flags, duplicated skills, free Team Captain Pro, star affiliations and paired hires, roster-specific inducements, rerolls/staff, gold, skill currency, and preset restrictions. Cloud saves validate these rules independently and reject violations, unknown catalog entries, and forged fields. An unfinished roster missing players or its required captain can be saved as a **draft**, with server-computed `legal: false`. Invalid edits remain in the local recovery draft until corrected. Advancement order and costs follow the selected exhibition/tournament ruleset; they do not represent earned league SPP or match history.

Squad-wide uniqueness, event registration, match-day inducements, special-play cards, variable-price mercenaries, and organiser rulings are outside scope. No league progression is implemented. Use an explicit snapshot version when changing factual rules. This app is in development: obsolete data can be discarded, and preserving old snapshots does not require migrations.
