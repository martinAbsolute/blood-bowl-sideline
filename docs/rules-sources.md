# Rules snapshot and provenance

Snapshot: `bb2025-2026-10-09`. Blood Bowl Third Season (2025). This is an independent community builder, not an organiser certification tool.

Current source precedence and exceptions are documented in [roster sources](roster-sources.md), [Star Player rules](star-player-rules.md), and [Sevens rules](sevens-rules.md). Source-derived regression fixtures retain individual profile URLs. Stable catalog IDs and the previous September snapshot remain readable; unknown rules versions are unsupported. Cloud reads recompute legality against current rules without rewriting historical league snapshots.

## Presets

Team special-rule help uses factual paraphrases of the BB2025 effects, cross-checked against [Blood Bowl Base: The Teams](https://bloodbowlbase.ru/bb2025/core_rules/the_teams/) and [Nuffle Zone: Special Rules](https://nufflezone.com/en/blood-bowl-news-en/special-rules-blood-bowl/). The Team Captain replacement timing follows the corrected commentary shown in the former reference. These descriptions and league reference pages do not change the saved ruleset validators, costs, or roster snapshot. League pages show league-affiliated and universal stars; god-specific eligibility remains in the shared team validator.

- **Default:** initial 1,000,000 GP draft budget, with additional skills priced at player-value costs. This is an editable exhibition draft; additional skills, stars, and inducements need organiser agreement for ordinary league recruitment. It does not establish a league team's match history or earned SPP.
- **Matched Play:** 1,150,000 GP; tier-based SP; one extra skill per player; at most four added copies of each individual elite skill, as clarified on page 3 of the [official May 2026 FAQ](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_20-05_blood_bowl_faq_errata-gytvlserev-ngihd3chox.pdf); tier-dependent secondary and star allowances. This differs from Sevens' two added elite skills total.
- **EuroBowl 2026:** BBTC revision `EB2026_04`, cross-checked with the Ukrainian builder: tier-based roster and skill-gold budgets, shared Flowing Funds, primary-only two-skill stacking, secondary and stack limits, restricted veteran/legend stars and advancement exclusions.
- **NAF World Cup 2027 v2.1:** the [organiser's v2.1 PDF](https://nafwc.com/app/uploads/NAF-World-Cup-Rules-V2.1.pdf), pages 2–4, governs roster budgets, SPP, ordered advancement costs, stacking and Star permissions, cumulative Star tax, eleven regular hires before Stars and the Secret Weapon bribe cap. BBTC identifies this preset as `WC2027_03`. Squad-wide Star uniqueness needs an organiser check across all six coaches.
- **Kyiv “Seven Sins” Sevens, Season 3:** organiser rules establish the 650,000 GP budget, tier skill packages, no skill stacking, no Leader, no stars, and tier-2 captain advancement exclusion. [Warhammer Community's Sevens designer notes](https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/f0owm2ar/designers-notes-on-blood-bowl-sevens/) confirm the four non-Lineman limit, universal 100,000 GP rerolls, and mandatory Lineman Veteran. Spike! Journal 22 drafting details were cross-checked against its [community transcription](https://bloodbowlbase.ru/bb2025/spike_journal/issue_22/): 7–11 players, Veteran MA reduced by one, Sevens staff prices and caps, and the restricted inducement list including Desperate Measures. The Veteran's once-per-game ability covers failed pickup, catch or pass rolls, and an own Block that would knock the Veteran down. [NAF BB2025 Sevens recommendations](https://www.thenaf.net/naf-recommendations-and-clarifications-for-bb2025/), Sevens revision 26 September 2026, apply: Insignificant is ignored during drafting; Team Captain retains starting Pro and may also be the Veteran. The event's Leader ban overrides NAF's general matched-play permission. NAF gameplay clarifications remain reference guidance; this roster builder does not enforce pitch actions, dice resolution, stalling or match results. Organiser decisions take precedence.

## What the checks cover

**Blood Bowl Sevens 2025 Edition:** the standard Spike! Journal 22 Matched Play draft, distinct from the Kyiv event: 600,000 GP, 7–11 players, four specialists, mandatory Lineman Veteran, and the Sevens staff and inducement costs above. Its four tiers grant 1/2/3/4 SP, with primary skills costing 1 SP and secondary skills 2 SP, at most one secondary, one added skill per player and two added elite skills. No Star Players appear in the eligible Sevens inducement list. Matched Play starts at zero Dedicated Fans, with up to five purchased at 20,000 GP each. NAF's clarification permits purchased Leader and Pro and retains Captain Pro. These rules cover a fixed Matched Play roster; they do not implement the distinct Sevens league advancement system. Sevens tiers follow Spike! Journal 22's [tier table](https://bloodbowlbase.ru/bb2025/spike_journal/issue_22/#sevens-team-tiers). Slann has no published GW Sevens tier and is excluded from the standard preset; it remains supported by the other presets.

Position limits, total players, Insignificant balance, shared Big Guy limits, skill access/categories/elite flags, duplicated skills, free Team Captain Pro, star affiliations and paired hires, roster-specific inducements, rerolls/staff, gold, skill currency, and preset restrictions. Cloud saves validate these rules independently and reject violations, unknown catalog entries, and forged fields. An unfinished roster missing players or its required captain can be saved as a **draft**, with server-computed `legal: false`. Invalid edits remain in the local recovery draft until corrected. Advancement order and costs follow the selected exhibition/tournament ruleset; they do not represent earned league SPP or match history.

Squad-wide uniqueness, event registration, match-day inducements, special-play cards, variable-price mercenaries, and organiser rulings are outside scope. These exhibition and tournament presets do not model league progression; sanctioned careers follow [league rules](league-rules-sources.md). Use an explicit snapshot version when changing factual rules.
